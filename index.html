Safety first
This plan is deliberately conservative following haemorrhoid banding around three months ago. It cannot guarantee prevention of recurrence. Avoid squats, deadlifts, heavy leg presses, maximal lifts, forceful bracing and breath-holding. Keep effort comfortable with about 3–4 reps left in reserve. Stop if you notice rectal/pelvic pressure, pain or bleeding, and contact your clinician. Seek urgent medical advice for significant bleeding or severe pain. Ask your treating clinician when it is appropriate to progress training.

Movement rules

  • Exhale through the effort; never deliberately bear down.
  • Use supported machines and light-to-moderate loads.
  • Move slowly and stop before form or breathing changes.
  • Skip any movement that creates pelvic or rectal pressure.
  • Use the visual guides as simplified cues, not a substitute for an in-person demonstration.

Apple Watch integration

This free web app cannot directly start an Apple Watch workout or read live HealthKit data. That requires a native iPhone/Watch app with Apple Health permissions. A future native version could record workout duration, heart rate and energy; reliable automatic rep counting would need separate exercise-specific detection and testing.
